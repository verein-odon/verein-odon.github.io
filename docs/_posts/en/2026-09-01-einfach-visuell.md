Explaining a UI problem to an AI chat tool in words alone is slow, and something always gets lost in translation. Our new tool, **einfach visuell** ("simply visual"), fixes that: drop a screenshot onto the canvas, drag icons onto it to point at exactly what you mean, then capture the result with your system's screenshot tool and share it.

Nothing leaves your browser. There's no account, no server, and nothing is uploaded or stored — the whole thing runs client-side. It's free to use, available in German and English, and the source code is open on GitHub under the EUPL-1.2 licence.
